Beit Iksa has no sewage treatment plant; Water Authority weighs options

The town has no wastewater treatment plant; the Water Authority is studying options.

No plant

The town has no wastewater treatment plant; the Water Authority is studying options.

The report

Water Authority head Ziad al-Mimi met Beit Iksa's mayor on 14 October about the town's sewage problem, given the lack of a wastewater treatment plant, and discussed network options. In a separate meeting he asked staff to study extra supply for Beit Iba, west of Nablus, to avoid a repeat of its earlier supply crisis.

Source

Palestinian Water Authority, via WAFA (Arabic). Event 14 Oct 2026; published 14 Oct 2026.
